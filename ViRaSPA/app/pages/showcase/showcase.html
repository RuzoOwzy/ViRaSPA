Entry table

Type a sale with the keyboard: Enter moves from cell to cell and adds a row at the end; Ctrl+Delete removes one. Amount is computed; totals follow.

Tree

A chart of accounts: arrows move, Right/Left open and close, Insert adds, F2 renames, Delete removes (only leaves), Ctrl+X then Ctrl+V moves; locked accounts can't change. Hover a row for the + and × buttons.

Rich text editor

Our own code: headings, formatting, lists (Tab indents), quote, alignment, links (Ctrl+K), images, undo/redo, clean paste, a count and a limit. Alt+F10 goes to the toolbar.
